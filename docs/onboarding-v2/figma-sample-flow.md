## Transcript (agent = grey, user = blue)

- **User:** Active Plow: ASDF2
- **Agent:** Look at you, jumping right in!
- **Agent:** [GIF: "QUICK Q"]
- **Agent:** What should I call you?
- **User:** Mary
- **Agent:** Hey Mary! I'm Elm 🍃
- **Agent:** Here's the thing, most AI can talk, I actually do things to keep your household on track: book the dentist, reorder the dog food before you run out, chase down the refund that's been "pending" for a month.
- **Agent:** The doing happens through an app on your Mac. That's what lets me act on your actual accounts instead of just talking about it.
- **Agent:** And since I run on your machine, not some server, your stuff stays yours: your logins sit in a vault I can use but never see, and nothing ever leaves your Mac. You set the boundaries, I work inside them. 🔒
- **Agent:** Want to see the kind of thing I mean?
- **Agent:** [3 Photos — screenshots of Plow working in a browser]
- **Agent:** Only catch — I'm not on your Mac yet. So right now I'm flying blind: no calendar, no inbox, nothing. Let's fix that.
- **Agent:** [Link card: Download Plow Latch — plow.co]
- **Agent:** Reach out anytime if you have a question during setup. I'm happy to help.
- *(annotation: "slight delay here (30s??)")*
- **Agent:** While you get the mac app setup, tell me about yourself.
- **Agent:** First, what city are you in? (Or a zip.) That tells me your timezone and lets me drop a weather read into your mornings.
- **User:** [city]
- **Agent:** Mountain View. ☀️ Pacific time — got it.
- **Agent:** Every morning I'll send a quick rundown: what's on the family's plate, the weather, anything I flagged overnight. When do you want it? Most folks say 7.
